## Goal

A stand-alone mod folder that appears in the SOMA launcher and starts its sample map.

## Requirements

SOMA installed, per [Prepare your tools](/start/prepare-your-tools/).

## Steps

Follow [Create and launch your mod](/start/create-and-launch-your-mod/) in order. That page is the checklist. Details: [Creating a mod](/modding/creating-a-mod/) and [MinimalCustomMapMod](/modding/minimalcustommapmod/).

## Expected result

The copied mod is in the launcher and the sample map runs.

## Why it works

The bundled example is a known-working `entry.hpc` + `resources.cfg` + map. Copying it avoids assembling those files from memory.

## Common failure

Editing the original example or the base game. Work in the copy.
