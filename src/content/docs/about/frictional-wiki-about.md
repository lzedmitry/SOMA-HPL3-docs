The **Frictional Games Wiki** is an online open-content collaborative resource; that is, a voluntary association of individuals and groups working to develop a common resource of the HPL engines. The platform allows anyone to alter its content, so please treat it as an open forum. Please be advised that nothing found here has necessarily been reviewed by people with the expertise required to provide you with complete, accurate or reliable information.

That is not to say that you will not find valuable and accurate information on the Frictional Games Wiki; most of the time you will. However, Frictional Games Wiki cannot guarantee the validity of the information found here. The content of any given article may recently have been changed or vandalized. This excludes protected articles written by Frictional Games staff.

This wiki aims to be:

- The definitive and most comprehensive source of information on using the HPL Engine and all of its iterations.
- A place for users to contribute their own ideas, tutorials, tools, as well as discuss them.
