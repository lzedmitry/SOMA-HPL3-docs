The [Amnesia: The Dark Descent Asset Pack](http://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=570600440) has been released on Steam.

You can download it and put it in your mod to use it, but it would be recommended to instead use a [mod dependency](/modding/mod-dependencies/). It might not be possible for Amnesia: Rebirth, because the asset pack is released as a SOMA mod.
