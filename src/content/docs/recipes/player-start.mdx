## Goal

The player appears at a chosen spot when the map loads.

## Requirements

Level Editor attached to your mod.

## Steps

1. Open the map in the Level Editor.
2. Create a **PlayerStart** Area where the player should appear. See [PlayerStart](/areas/playerstart-area/) for the properties the Wiki lists.
3. Save the `.hpm` and its sidecar files.
4. Launch the mod.

## Expected result

You spawn in that volume.

## Why it works

PlayerStart is the documented spawn volume.

## Common failure

Editor still on base-game resources. Title bar must say `(Working on mod)`.
